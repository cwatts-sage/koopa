# Watts Football Pool — Rules

## Format
- **Straight up picks** — pick the winner of each game, no point spread
- 1 point per correct pick

## Schedule
- **NFL Regular Season** (18 weeks, Sept 2026 – Jan 2027)
- Preseason dry run in August 2026 (optional, doesn't count)

## Weekly Flow

### 1. Matchups Posted (Tuesday/Wednesday)
- WFC posts the week's matchups to the group chat
- Format: numbered list of games (e.g., "1. Cowboys @ Eagles")

### 2. Picks Submitted (via DM)
- Members **DM their picks privately** to Toadstool's iMessage
- Format: just send the team names or numbers from the matchup list
- Example: "Cowboys, Chiefs, Bills, Broncos" or "1, 3, 5, 7"
- Picks are **secret** until results are posted

### 3. Deadline
- **All picks due before the first game of the week**
- Typically Thursday night (TNF kickoff) unless otherwise noted
- Late/missing picks = no points for those games

### 4. Results Posted (Monday/Tuesday)
- After all games are played, WFC posts:
  - Correct answers
  - Each member's score for the week
  - Updated season standings

## Standings
- Cumulative points across all weeks
- Tiebreaker: head-to-head record (TBD if needed)
