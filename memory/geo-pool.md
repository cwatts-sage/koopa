## Gotchas learned
- **AccuBlue CH readings can be badly wrong.** 09-05 reported CH 801; 09-07 read 494.
  Always confirm an outlier with a second test before acting. Nearly recommended a drain.
- **CYA inflates the TA reading.** At CYA 84, ~28 ppm of measured TA is cyanurate.
  Subtract it before running LSI — carbonate alk is the number that matters.
