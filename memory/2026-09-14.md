## Geo — daughter's student loan consolidation (advisory thread, 2026-09-14)

**Situation:** Geo consolidating his daughter's 8 federal loans via NelNet. As of 2026-07-01
only two repayment plans are selectable: **Tiered Standard** (now the default) or **RAP
(Repayment Assistance Plan)**. Standard Plan is gone.

**Her numbers:**
- Balance: **$20,321**
- AGI (last year): **$82,045**
- Loan types: **Direct Subsidized/Unsubsidized** (confirmed on studentaid.gov)
- Rates: 2 @ 4.53%, 2 @ 4.99%, 4 @ 5.5% (per-loan balances not provided)
- Public service / PSLF: **No**

**Recommendation given: consolidate → Tiered Standard.** Reasoning:
- Debt is only ~25% of annual income; she doesn't need income-driven relief
- Tiered Standard ≈ $200–235/mo, 10–12 yr term
- RAP at $82K AGI ≈ $550–680/mo and forgives nothing — wrong tool for this ratio
- No PSLF angle, so RAP's only unique advantage is irrelevant
- Direct loans forfeit nothing on consolidation
- Weighted avg rate ≈ 5.13% → rounds up to ~5.25% (bracket 4.625%–5.5%). Rounding
  penalty is ~$12–25 over the life of the loan. Negligible.
- Suggested autopay (-0.25%) and extra principal payments (~$150/mo extra → ~6 yrs)

**Scare that turned out to be nothing:** Geo initially read the summary as "all Perkins."
Flagged hard STOP — consolidating Perkins permanently forfeits Perkins cancellation
(teacher/nurse/first-responder etc.). Also noted Perkins were discontinued 2017-18 and are
held by the school, not NelNet, so the labeling looked wrong. He re-checked studentaid.gov:
they're Direct Sub/Unsub. Green light.

**Lesson:** always make them verify loan type on studentaid.gov directly, not from the
servicer's summary page. Servicer summaries mislabel.

**Caveat I gave:** no web_search provider on this box — couldn't pull live 2026 RAP bracket
tables. Told him to confirm via studentaid.gov/loan-simulator before submitting. Gap between
the two plans is wide enough that the recommendation holds even if my RAP estimate is off 30%.

**Status:** Geo said "Ok" — going to submit. No open action for me.
